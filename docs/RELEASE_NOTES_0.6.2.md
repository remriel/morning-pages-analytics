# Remember the writing-history range

History settings now offer **Last used** alongside the 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365-day, and All dates opening ranges. With Last used selected, reopening Writing history restores the previous preset, navigated period, or custom date interval. A chosen default preset takes precedence when the view opens. Existing saved defaults remain in place; new installs use Last used and begin at 30 days until another range is chosen.

The opening-range setting applies to newly opened history views, leaving a view already open on the range you are exploring.
